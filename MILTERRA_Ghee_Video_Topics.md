# MILTERRA ghee video topics

**Audience:** People comparing ghee for their households, including buyers who care about ingredients, sourcing, and food safety.  
**Purpose:** Answer purchase questions with real process footage, measurements, and verifiable evidence.  
**Status key:** Idea → Researching → Filmed → Edited → Published.  
**Current call to action:** Follow the experiments and subscribe for launch updates. Do not imply that a product or batch is available for purchase before it is.

## 30–45 second video structure

1. **0–3 seconds — A real buyer question:** “Milk makes you feel bloated. Is all dairy the same?”
2. **3–12 seconds — Clarify the issue:** Separate lactose intolerance from cow's-milk allergy. Do not diagnose the viewer.
3. **12–25 seconds — Show what you can prove:** Film the actual batch, source records, measurements, or a relevant lab result.
4. **25–35 seconds — Honest takeaway:** Say what the evidence establishes and where it stops.
5. **35–45 seconds — Call to action:** Invite viewers to follow batch experiments and receive launch updates.

## Editorial checks before publishing

- Use actual footage and figures from the batch being discussed. Label demonstrations and simulations clearly.
- Describe bilona as a **production method**; do not promise that it cures acidity, IBS, inflammation, or other conditions.
- Do not say cream-made ghee lacks butyric acid. A published comparison detected it in both curd-butter and cream-butter ghee.
- Distinguish **lactose intolerance** from **cow's-milk allergy**; ghee is still milk-derived.
- Do not call ghee an essential first food for babies or promise brain-development benefits.
- Do not claim all refined vegetable oils contain industrial trans fats or that ghee is a heart-health treatment.
- Check claims such as “A2”, “organic”, “pesticide-free”, and vitamin content against the evidence for the **specific milk and product**.
- Show what a lab test actually measured; do not turn a single test into a blanket “100% pure” claim.
- Keep purchase or waitlist language accurate to the current stage of the project.
