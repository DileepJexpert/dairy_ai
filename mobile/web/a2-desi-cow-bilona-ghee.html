Why Authentic Vedic Bilona Ghee Matters

100% Cultured Curd vs Commercial Cream

Commercial industrial ghee is made by centrifuging raw milk cream at high heat. Milterra Bilona Ghee is prepared strictly from whole cultured A2 curd, producing probiotic makkhan with rich aroma and granular danedar texture.

Indigenous Sahiwal Cows (Bos Indicus)

Sourced purely from indigenous Indian hump cows known for A2 beta-casein protein, free of the inflammatory A1 mutant proteins found in commercial hybrid European breeds.

Ahinsa & Cruelty-Free Ethos

We respect natural maternal bonds. The calf is always fed its full share of milk before gentle hand-milking takes place. No synthetic hormones, oxytocin, or antibiotics.
