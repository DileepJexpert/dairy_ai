Frequently Asked Questions

How long does MILTERRA fresh paneer stay fresh?

Because our paneer is completely free of chemical preservatives, it has a shelf life of 4-5 days when kept refrigerated between 2°C to 4°C. We recommend consuming it fresh or freezing in an airtight container for up to 15 days.

Why does your paneer not turn rubbery when pan-fried?

Commercial paneer turns hard or rubbery when starch and skimmed milk powders overheat. Since Milterra paneer contains natural cow milk fat and moisture, it sears with a golden crust outside while staying soft and juicy inside.

Is this paneer suitable for people with mild lactose sensitivity?

Most customers with sensitivity to A1 cow milk find A2 Sahiwal paneer easy to digest because A2 beta-casein does not produce BCM-7 peptides. Additionally, the curdling and whey separation process naturally reduces lactose levels.
