Our Laboratory Purity Standards

We believe trust should not be taken on faith alone. Every batch of MILTERRA Ghee, Oil, and Honey undergoes testing by NABL-accredited food testing laboratories.

1. A2 Beta-Casein DNA Testing

Real-time PCR genetic testing verifying 100% homozygous A2A2 allele presence in our milk pools, ensuring zero contamination from foreign A1 cow breeds.

✓ 100% PURE A2A2 CONFIRMED

2. Reichert-Meissl (RM) Value

Measures volatile water-soluble fatty acids. FSSAI requires RM value above 28.0; MILTERRA Desi Cow Ghee consistently tests between 31.5 to 33.2.

✓ RM VALUE > 31.0 (PASSED)

3. Baudouin & Halphen Tests

Specific chromogenic chemical tests to detect adulteration with cheaper vanaspati (hydrogenated vegetable fat), palm oil, or mineral oils.

✓ ZERO PALM / HYDROGENATED OIL

4. Nuclear Magnetic Resonance (NMR)

NMR spectroscopy profiling used on our Raw Mustard Honey to detect even 0.1% synthetic C3/C4 corn, rice, or beet sugar syrup adulteration.

✓ ZERO ADDED SYRUPS DETECTED

5. 120-Pesticide Residue Screen

Gas Chromatography-Mass Spectrometry (GC-MS) screening for synthetic organophosphates, carbamates, and glyphosate in raw pasture grasses.

✓ BELOW DETECTION LIMIT (<0.01 mg/kg)

6. Heavy Metal ICP-MS Testing

Inductively Coupled Plasma Mass Spectrometry verification to ensure zero lead (Pb), mercury (Hg), arsenic (As), or cadmium in our Lakadong turmeric and ghee.

✓ 100% NON-DETECTABLE
