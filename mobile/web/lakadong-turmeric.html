Frequently Asked Questions

Why is Lakadong turmeric significantly darker and redder than grocery store haldi?

The deep reddish-golden color is the direct visual proof of exceptionally high natural curcumin (>7%). Industrial grocery turmeric looks bright pale yellow because curcumin has been extracted for pharmaceutical use or because it has been diluted with starch.

How should I consume Lakadong turmeric for maximum absorption?

Curcumin is lipophilic (fat-soluble). To maximize bodily absorption, consume it alongside healthy fats—such as 1 teaspoon in warm A2 Cow Milk with 1/2 tsp of A2 Cow Ghee and a pinch of black pepper (piperine).
