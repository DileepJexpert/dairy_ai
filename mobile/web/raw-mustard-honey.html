Frequently Asked Questions

Why has my honey turned thick, white, or crystallized?

Crystallization is the hallmark of genuine, unpasteurized honey! Mustard blossom honey naturally has a higher glucose-to-fructose ratio, causing it to crystallize into a rich, spreadable cream. Commercial honeys stay clear only because they are boiled and stripped of pollen. If you prefer it runny, place the glass jar in a warm water bath (below 40°C).

Is this honey tested for added sugar syrups?

Yes. Every batch undergoes NMR (Nuclear Magnetic Resonance) spectroscopy and SMR/TMR isotope testing to ensure absolute zero presence of Chinese rice syrups, corn syrups, or inverted cane sugar.

Can I mix raw honey in hot boiling tea or milk?

In Ayurveda, heating honey above 40°C is strictly advised against as it turns 'Ama' (toxic) and destroys active enzymes. Always stir raw honey into beverages after they have cooled down to comfortable drinking temperature.
