Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Dispatch & Fulfillment Timelines

All orders placed on MILTERRA Foods are hand-packed at our fulfillment hub within 24 to 36 business hours. We do not dispatch during public holidays or regional transport strikes.

2. Shipping Charges & Free Delivery

  • Orders above ₹999: Free standard shipping across all serviceable pin codes in India.
  • Orders below ₹999: Flat shipping fee of ₹75 applies at checkout.
  • Express Air Delivery: Available for select tier-1 metro cities at ₹150 surcharge.

3. Estimated Transit Times

  • Metro Cities (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata): 2 to 3 business days.
  • Tier-2 & Tier-3 Cities: 3 to 5 business days.
  • Northeast, Jammu & Kashmir, Remote Outposts: 5 to 7 business days.

4. Glass-Safe transit Packaging

Our ghee, oils, and raw honey are bottled in glass jars. Each bottle is wrapped in multi-layer biodegradable honeycomb paper buffering and packed into heavy 5-ply corrugated outer boxes with tamper-evident security tape.

5. In-Transit Damage & Breakage Guarantee

In the rare event of transit damage or leakage:

  • Take a photo or quick unboxing video of the damaged package.
  • Send it via WhatsApp to our Care Desk (+91 91702 36792) or email care@milterrafoods.com within 48 hours of delivery.
  • We will dispatch a 100% free replacement immediately without requiring you to return broken glass.

6. Tracking Your Order

Once dispatched, an automated SMS and WhatsApp notification containing your courier AWB tracking number (Delhivery / Bluedart / Shiprocket) will be sent to the phone number provided at checkout.
